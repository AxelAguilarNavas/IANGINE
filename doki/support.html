Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where are my documents and data stored?

Doki works 100% locally on your phone. No personal documents or metadata are ever transmitted to IANGINE servers or any third-party clouds.

What happens if I forget my PIN?

If you enabled biometrics (Face ID, Touch ID, or Android Biometric Prompt), you can use it to unlock the app. Because of our offline, zero-knowledge architecture, IANGINE cannot reset your PIN remotely.

How can I back up my documents?

You can export an encrypted .doki backup file from Profile/Settings. Be sure to remember the password you set, as IANGINE cannot recover password-protected backups.

How can I delete all my data?

You can purge all stored data anytime from Profile → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the application from your phone.
