Doki Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who we are

Doki is an application developed by [[exact legal entity of IANGINE, matching Google Play and Apple developer accounts]] ("IANGINE", "we", "us"). Contact: [[support@iangine.com or other confirmed email]].

2. Summary

Doki stores your documents only on your phone. We do not have servers that receive your documents or your data. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

3. What information the app handles

The app stores on your device, in encrypted form: the files you add (photos, scans, images, and PDFs), the data you enter about them (name, number, category, cardholder/holder, expiration dates, and reminders), your settings, and your name. This information never leaves your phone unless you choose to share it (see section 6).

4. Security

Files and the database are encrypted on your device. The encryption key is stored in the system secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). You can protect access with a 6-digit PIN and biometrics (fingerprint or facial recognition). The app does not receive your biometric data: it is processed entirely by the operating system.

5. Permissions the app requests

  • Camera: to take photos or scan documents.
  • Notifications: to alert you before a document expires. Scheduled locally on your phone.
  • Biometrics: to unlock the app, if enabled.
  • Files and photos: to choose a file you want to save, using the system file picker.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your phone's system settings.

6. When information leaves your phone

Only when you explicitly choose to:

  • Sharing a document: Doki sends the file through the system share sheet. Once it leaves Doki, it is no longer protected by the app; the app warns you before doing so.
  • Backup: you can export an encrypted .doki file protected by a password you choose. We cannot recover this password: without it, the backup cannot be opened.
  • Receiving files from other apps: (for example, via WhatsApp or email) uses the system share menu.

7. VIP plan survey

Doki may invite you to answer a survey regarding a potential VIP plan. Tapping "Take survey" opens your browser to an external website (sigmasurvs.com). That website is independent of Doki and maintains its own privacy policy. The app transmits no data when opening it. [[Confirm: Does IANGINE receive and process survey responses? If so, describe what data and for what purpose.]]

8. What Doki does not do

There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party tools sending data. The app does not request internet access permission.

9. Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your phone until you delete it. You can delete it from Profile → Delete all data or by uninstalling the app. If you uninstall without having a backup, it cannot be recovered.
